# Your household evidence workflow

1. Get a private invitation from the operator. Use its assigned email and code to create your account. Save the one-time recovery code securely.
2. Upload your own PDF, PNG, or JPEG bill. The original is saved privately before document reading starts. If processing fails, use manual entry or retry. Unattached uploads can be resumed or removed from Documents.
3. Compare each candidate field with the document. Correct mistakes, identify the meter arrangement and reading basis, and confirm the record. OCR confidence does not prove a value is correct.
4. Follow the finding's next action. Inspect a bill, add the consecutive previous month, resolve revised versions, or enter a genuine dated observation. Never invent a past meter reading to make a comparison available.
5. Keep payment amounts and dates with the correct bill, with optional receipt evidence. This does not verify payment posting by the utility.
6. Open a case when a source difference needs investigation. Its calculation, sources, and rule version are captured permanently. The checklist, notes, status, and outcome are your own reports.
7. Download the private PDF to retain or share yourself. Downloading does not submit a complaint. Later bill edits do not alter the captured calculation.
8. Use Settings to renew a lost recovery code after confirming your password, or delete your account and its live records and documents. Failed storage deletion stays pending and can be retried.

Unknown reading basis, missing dates, changed meters, unsupported arrangements, gaps, and duplicate versions can prevent a comparison. An unavailable check is not a clean bill, and a finding is not proof of utility error. English PDF export replaces unsupported characters with question marks and states that limitation; the HTML report retains the original text.
